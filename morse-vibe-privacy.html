Morse Vibe — Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Short version: Morse Vibe does not collect, store, or share any personal data. Everything stays on your device.

Who We Are

Morse Vibe is developed by FaithAlarm. Contact: faithalarmapp@gmail.com

Data We Collect

We do not collect any personal data. The following is stored locally on your device only and never transmitted to us:

App settings · Message history (last 10 messages) · Room ID for Remote tab · Encryption key if set

Permissions

Microphone — Used by the Listen tab to decode morse audio. Never recorded or transmitted.

Camera — Used by Flash, Image and QR tabs. Processed on-device only.

Bluetooth — Used by Nearby tab to find other Morse Vibe users. No data sent to our servers.

Internet — Used by Remote tab to relay messages. Messages are not stored on our server. Encryption available.

Third-Party Services

ADS-B.lol — The Air tab fetches live public flight data. No personal data is sent.

Children

Morse Vibe does not knowingly collect data from children under 13.
